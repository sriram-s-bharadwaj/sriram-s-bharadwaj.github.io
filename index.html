Research

My research focuses on quantum algorithms and the quantum simulation of strongly interacting many-body systems. My work on formal aspects of quantum field theory informs my approach to constructing physically faithful and resource-efficient Hamiltonian formulations.

Quantum simulation of many-body systems and lattice gauge theories

I develop Hamiltonian formulations suited to quantum simulation and study their phase structure and dynamics using tensor networks, neural quantum states, exact diagonalization, and variational quantum algorithms.

Recent work includes confinement and screening in the Schwinger model in curved space using MPS/DMRG, as well as quantum simulations of topological phases in (2+1)D lattice QED.

Quantum algorithms and optimization

I study quantum algorithms for continuous optimization and ground-state preparation, with an emphasis on rigorous performance guarantees and applications to many-body systems.

Recent work includes CV-QAOA, for which we establish convergence guarantees and black-box quantum-classical separations, and adiabatic algorithms for bosonic and lattice gauge theory Hamiltonians with provable error and runtime guarantees.
